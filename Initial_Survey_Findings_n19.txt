LIMITATIONS
Small, uneven sample: university students form most responses; Nagarparkar has the largest taluka group. Results are not representative of all District Tharparkar. Multi-select questions count each option selected, so totals can exceed the number of respondents. These are perceptions, not verified school inspections or causal findings. Raw emails, timestamps and free-text answers are excluded from website charts and public aggregate files.
