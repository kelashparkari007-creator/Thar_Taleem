INITIAL SURVEY FINDINGS — 23 OCTOBER 2026 (n=11; preliminary, non-representative)
An initial Google Forms export contained 11 responses. Respondents selected Nagarparkar (7), Mithi (2), Islamkot (1) and Diplo (1); 9 described their area as rural, 1 urban and 1 town/qasba. Ten respondents were university students and one was a parent/guardian. This sample is therefore not representative of all Tharparkar taluqas or community groups.
School access: 5 of 11 described access as 'Mushkil' or 'Bohat mushkil'. In the multiple-select facilities question, computer lab/internet was selected by 9 respondents, books/learning material by 8, facilities suitable for girls by 8, clean drinking water by 7, classrooms/buildings by 7, and clean toilets by 7. For possible reasons children may not complete education, financial hardship was selected by 9, lack of education/career guidance by 8, and distance/transport by 6. Career guidance was described as often unavailable by 5 respondents; another 5 said it is available to some extent. Digital access was reported as very limited by 5 and unavailable by 4. Six respondents said yes and five said maybe to a free career/computer-skills session.
Interpretation and limitations: These are respondents' perceptions, not verified school inspections or evidence of causation. Multiple-select counts can exceed 11. Increase and diversify the sample across all taluqas, rural/urban areas, respondent roles and genders before making district-wide claims. Do not publish emails or raw individual answers.
